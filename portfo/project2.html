Surviving in Korea

Founder & Leader · 4 years

Overview

I founded and led "Surviving in Korea" for four years — a club dedicated to helping multicultural children and foreigners settle into life in Korea. We shared practical guidance and gave kids a consistent, welcoming space to learn and grow.

What I Did

  • Taught multicultural children every Saturday at Dongsan Church.
  • Organized special events, like a Quiz Bowl during Christmas.
  • Managed the club's official Instagram account.
  • Led the club's direction and activities across four years.
