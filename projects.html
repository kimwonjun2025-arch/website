English Phrase Trainer

Interactive Tool · Built with JavaScript

When English isn't your first language, the hardest part isn't grammar — it's the casual phrases and everyday words that textbooks never teach, and the context behind them. I built this flashcard tool to make those easier to pick up: flip cards for the meaning and a real example, shuffle through them, and hear the pronunciation.
